About & credits

Roots lets you explore English words through the Greek and Latin pieces they are built from. Every page is generated by a small script from freely licensed data; the code is on GitHub. It is a sister site of the Sino-Japanese word explorer.

Word data: Wiktionary

Definitions, etymologies and word breakdowns are adapted from the English Wiktionary, written by its contributors and available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence (and the GFDL). Every word and root links back to its Wiktionary entry, where the full text and its history of authors can be found.

The Wiktionary data was read from the machine-readable dumps at kaikki.org, made with Wiktextract: Tatu Ylonen, Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured Data, Proceedings of LREC 2022.

Word frequencies: wordfreq

Difficulty levels use word frequencies from wordfreq by Robyn Speer (data CC BY-SA 4.0, code Apache 2.0).

What was changed

Definitions are shortened to the first one or two senses. Roots, spelling groups, word families, "auto-split" breakdowns, Greek/Latin labels, levels, neighbourhoods and the map are worked out by this site and are not part of the original data, so they can contain mistakes that Wiktionary doesn't.
