How are levels decided?

Levels come from how often a word appears in everyday English (books, news, subtitles, the web), using the wordfreq word lists:

  • Everyday — words almost everyone knows
  • Common — words you meet often in news and books
  • Educated — words a well-read adult knows
  • Specialist — words mostly used in a field: science, medicine, law…
  • Rare — words you will hardly ever meet

A root's level is the level of its most common word, so tele is Everyday because of television.
