Root neighbourhoods

Think of every root as a town and every word as roads joining its roots. Nearly all roots end up in one big web, and the web falls into neighbourhoods: groups of roots that build far more words with each other than with the rest. The groups were found automatically (Louvain community detection); the names are hand-picked to fit. See them on the map.
